Federation Technical Regulations

All competitive yoga activities conducted under the aegis of the Telangana Yoga Association comply strictly with the official technical handbook of the Yoga Federation of India.

Holding Duration: Traditional postures require 15 to 30 seconds of undisturbed hold.
Choreography & Music: Artistic & Rhythmic sequences must adhere to non-vocal classical tempos.
Scrutiny Standards: Mandatory verification of Date of Birth to preserve competitive age group integrity.

5 Championship Competition Categories

1. Traditional Yogasana

Classical static postures evaluated for alignment, stillness, spine angle, breathing ease, and facial serenity.

2. Artistic Yogasana (Solo)

Expressive performance linking postures through graceful transitions to harmonious musical rhythm.

3. Artistic Yogasana (Pair)

Two athletes executing complementary postures, partner lifts, symmetric balances, and synchronized transitions.

4. Rhythmic Yogasana (Pair)

Continuous fluid transitions matching steady tempo without stopping or breaking postural rhythm.

5. Free Flow

Creative flowing sequences harmonizing breath control, flexibility transitions, and dynamic balance.
