Association Membership & Affiliation

Distinguishing association membership from tournament registration. Learn about approved categories for practitioners, coaches, athletes, and institutions.

Important Distinction: Association Membership vs. Event Registration

Championship Registration is open to any qualifying athlete nominated from Telangana for a specific tournament. In contrast, Association Membership represents permanent or annual organizational affiliation with the Telangana Yoga Association for instructors, schools, clubs, and patrons.

Proposed Categories

Approved Membership Structures

Categories established under the association's bylaws to foster broad community involvement and accredited standards.

Athlete Member

Competitive Practitioners

For competitive yogasana athletes seeking permanent state roster tracking, eligibility for state training camps, and national trials ranking logs.

  • Admit card tracking
  • Trial eligibility notice

Coach / Judge Member

Certified Adjudicators

For qualified yoga teachers and trainers desiring inclusion in the accredited State Technical Officials directory and referee examination panels.

  • Referee refreshers
  • Official panel listing

Institutional Member

Schools & Academies

For registered yoga ashrams, private clubs, sports academies, and recognized schools participating regularly in inter-district activities.

  • Direct school nomination
  • Annual affiliation certificate

Life / Patron Member

Eminent Yoga Enthusiasts

For distinguished yoga veterans, patrons, and benefactors contributing to the long-term infrastructure and promotion of yoga in Telangana.

  • Honorary invitation
  • Advisory council engagement

Membership Scrutiny & Verification Protocol

Membership applications are reviewed on a rolling basis by the Membership & Credentials Committee. Submissions require verified identity proofs, credentials verification, and formal approval by the General Secretariat.

Step 1: Expression of Interest

Submit basic details and category choice via official enquiry form or district chapter.

Step 2: Scrutiny of Documents

Verification of coaching diplomas, institutional credentials, or athlete history.

Step 3: Executive Ratification

Issuance of membership accreditation number and inclusion in state records.

To submit an application query or check current affiliation status, contact the secretariat. Inquire About Membership
