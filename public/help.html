Help Center & Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information on age category cut-offs, acceptable Date of Birth proof documents, admit cards, competition disciplines, and nomination procedures.

Topics: Age & Cut-offs DOB Documents Hall Tickets Disciplines & Rules School & District Entries Helplines

1. Age Categories & Cut-Off Date Rules

Official Yoga Federation calculation criteria and competition age divisions.

How is an athlete's competition age category calculated?

As per Yoga Federation regulations, competition age is calculated based on the athlete's year of birth relative to the championship reference date of December 31st of the competition year:

Sub-Junior:
Ages 8 to under 14
(08–10 Yrs & 10–14 Yrs)
Junior:
Ages 14 to under 18
(14–18 Yrs)
Senior:
Ages 18 and above
(18–25, 25–35, Above 35)
What is the minimum age to participate in the championship?

The strict minimum age is 8 completed years. Athletes below 8 years of age are not eligible for state-level championship competitions to ensure child physical safety and appropriate athletic development. The portal registration automatically checks date of birth and disallows submissions under 8 years.

What if an athlete turns 14 during the championship calendar year?

Because the cutoff date is December 31st, an athlete who attains 14 years of age on or before December 31st of the competition year is considered 14 years old and competes in the Junior Division (14–18 Yrs), not the Sub-Junior division.

2. Approved Proof of Date of Birth Documents

Government and institutional certificates accepted for athlete scrutiny.

Which documents are officially recognized as proof of Date of Birth?

The Technical Scrutiny Committee accepts any one of the following 4 documents:

  • Municipal / Gram Panchayat Birth Certificate: Issued under the Registration of Births and Deaths Act (Form 5).
  • School Bonafide / Study Certificate: On official institution letterhead, signed and sealed by the Headmaster / Principal, stating the student's full name, father's name, admission number, and recorded Date of Birth.
  • SSC / 10th Class Board Certificate / Transfer Certificate (T.C.): Official state board marksheet or school transfer certificate with stamped date of birth.
  • Aadhaar Card: Official Aadhaar card bearing complete Day/Month/Year of birth.
Why does the portal only request the Last 4 Digits of Aadhaar?

In compliance with UIDAI guidelines, Supreme Court rulings on privacy, and India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the Telangana Yoga Association practices data minimization:

We never collect, store, or transmit the full 12-digit Aadhaar number. The last 4 digits are used strictly for in-person identification at the venue, preventing identity duplication while protecting minor athlete privacy. Review our Privacy Policy for full details.

What if the athlete's name differs slightly across documents?

If minor spelling discrepancies exist (e.g. initials expanded or surname order), submit an official bonafide letter from the school Principal confirming both names refer to the same student, or present a self-declaration countersigned by your District Yoga Association Secretary during document scrutiny.

3. Hall Tickets (Admit Cards) & Venue Entry

How to retrieve, verify, and present admit cards on championship day.

How do I find and reprint an athlete's Admit Card if lost?

You can search and reprint an admit card at any time through the official lookup page:

  1. Navigate to Admit Card Lookup.
  2. Enter the official Chest Number (e.g. HYD-JR-01) or MongoDB Athlete Identifier.
  3. Click Print Admit Card to download or print directly. No login password is required for public admit card printing.
What are the reporting times and venue entry requirements?

Athletes must report to the registration scrutiny desk at least 30 minutes prior to the scheduled batch call for their age division.

Please carry:

  • Printed copy of the official Admit Card with photograph.
  • Original Date of Birth proof or school photo identity card.
  • Yogasana competition attire adhering to federation dress guidelines.

4. Competition Disciplines & Scoring Regulations

Official championship events, posture holding durations, and penalties.

What competitive yogasana disciplines are conducted?
  • Traditional Yogasana: Compulsory and optional syllabus postures evaluated on breathing stability, posture retention, and alignment.
  • Artistic Yogasana Solo: Choreographed asana sequence accompanied by background music showcasing flexibility, strength, balance, and aesthetic grace.
  • Artistic Yogasana Pair: Synchronized routine executed by two athletes (same gender) with partner lifts and symmetry.
  • Rhythmic Yogasana Pair: Dynamic flowing postures executed without static pause in rhythm to continuous instrumental tempo.
  • Free Flow Yogasana: Continuous sequential expression of classical asana transitions with breath control.
What is the posture holding time for Traditional Yogasana?

In Traditional Yogasana, each selected asana must be held in final stillness for a minimum of 30 seconds. Tremors, losing balance, touching the floor prematurely, or inability to hold the posture results in negative score deductions by the panel of judges.

5. School Delegations, District Entries & Fees

Entry fee guidelines, bulk registration workflows, and payment confirmation.

What is the official championship registration fee?

The official registration fee is ₹260 per registered event discipline. If an athlete registers for 2 disciplines (e.g. Traditional Yogasana and Artistic Solo), the total entry fee is ₹520. Fee receipts are generated with the official Admit Card.

How can schools register their full student delegation?

Schools and academies should use the dedicated School Nomination Portal:

  1. Enter the school/college name, district, Physical Education Teacher (PET) / Coach contact, and Principal name.
  2. Add student athlete entries (up to 50 students per delegation submission).
  3. Upload student passport photos and verified bonafide/DOB certificates.
  4. Confirm the Institutional Certification & DPDP Act 2023 Parental Consent declaration.
  5. Admit cards with unique sequential chest numbers are instantly generated for all students.
