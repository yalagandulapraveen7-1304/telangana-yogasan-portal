Terms & Conditions of Participation

Statutory conditions governing athlete nomination, technical rules compliance, eligibility verification, entry credentials, and association membership.

1 Acceptance of Terms

By accessing, browsing, submitting athlete nominations, or utilizing administrative portals of the Telangana Yoga Association ("Association", "we", "us"), participants, registered District Secretaries, institutions, coaches, and legal guardians agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. These terms operate in tandem with the official Yoga Federation of India rules and the Association's Privacy Policy.

2 Eligibility & Age Group Compliance

Athlete nominations are accepted exclusively for bona fide residents or students of the 33 administrative districts of Telangana. Every athlete's competition category is strictly governed by their verified Date of Birth as of the December 31st cut-off date:

  • Sub-Junior: 8 years completed to under 14 years.
  • Junior: 14 years completed to under 18 years.
  • Senior: 18 years completed and above.

Any falsification of birth records, duplicate Aadhaar numbers, or fraudulent identity uploads will result in immediate disqualification, nullification of awards, and potential disciplinary ban under State Federation bylaws.

3 Parental & Guardian Consent for Minor Athletes

In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, institutional nominators and District Secretaries must warrant that verifiable parental or legal guardian consent has been obtained prior to submitting data, certificates, or photographs of any minor athlete aged below 18 years.

4 Registration Fees & Financial Transactions

Where applicable, championship entry and processing fees (statutorily calculated per competition event) are processed securely via certified payment gateways.

  • Nomination processing fees are non-refundable once an official Chest Number and Admit Card have been generated.
  • In the event of duplicate debit resulting from gateway timeout, refunds will be reconciled within 5 to 7 business banking days upon written notice to the secretariat.

5 Adjudication, Scoring & Disciplinary Protocol

All competitive yogasana events are judged in accordance with Yoga Federation of India syllabus specifications. Decisions made by the official Jury of Appeal and Chief Referee regarding posture execution, holding timing, penalty deductions, and merit rankings are final and binding. Unsportsmanlike conduct by athletes, coaches, or spectators may result in venue eviction and suspension.

6 Secretariat Contact & Grievance Redressal

For legal inquiries, procedural clarifications, or official grievance notifications, contact the Association Secretariat:
