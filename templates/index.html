Competitive Formats

Official Championship Disciplines

Competitions are conducted under official National Yogasana Sports standards with standardized time limits, asana syllabi, and scoring rubrics.

Traditional Yogasana

Solo performance of compulsory & optional classical asanas with strict holding time (minimum 15-30 secs), steady breathing, and perfect poise.

Holding • Form • Alignment

Artistic Yogasana (Solo)

Choreographed sequence of postures performed to classical instrumental music, demonstrating flexibility, strength, dynamic balance, and rhythm.

Choreography • Music • Grace

Artistic Yogasana (Pair)

Two athletes of the same gender executing synchronized and complementary yogic postures, including partner lifts and symmetric transitions.

Synchronization • Partner Lift

Rhythmic Yogasana (Pair)

Dynamic pair composition matching continuous musical tempo. Seamless flow transitions between asanas without breaks in posture continuity.

Fluid Transition • Tempo Match

Free Flow

It is a continuous sequence of yoga postures performed smoothly and rhythmically, linking one asana to the next with controlled breathing and graceful movements.

Flow • Alignment • Breathing-control
Participation Criteria

Eligibility & Age Divisions

Athletes must be bona fide residents or students of their nominated Telangana district. Age category is determined strictly by verified Date of Birth.

🌱
Category 1

Sub-Junior Division

08 to <14 Years
  • Group A: 08–10 Yrs | Group B: 10–14 Yrs
  • Must have completed 8 years of age
  • School bonafide & Municipal Birth Certificate
Events: Traditional, Artistic Solo & Pair, Rhythmic, Free Flow
⚡
Category 2

Junior Division

14 to <18 Years
  • Merging of 14–16 Yrs & 16–18 Yrs
  • Must have completed 14 years of age
  • Aadhaar Card + School / College ID mandatory
Events: All Championship Disciplines
🏆
Category 3

Senior Division

18 Years & Above
  • Groups: 18–25 Yrs, 25–35 Yrs & Above 35 Yrs
  • Must have completed 18 years of age
  • District residency proof + Aadhaar Card
Events: All Championship Disciplines (Men & Women)
Official Age Computation Rule: Athletes will be automatically classified into their respective age divisions via the nomination system based on their official Date of Birth. Any submission with forged age documents will lead to lifetime debarment by the State Disciplinary Committee.

About the Telangana Yoga Association

The Telangana Yoga Association is an independent sports and cultural organization committed to the promotion, practice, and systematic development of Yoga and competitive Yogasana Sports across Telangana.

Affiliated with the Yoga Federation of India, the association coordinates state championships, athlete development camps, and certified technical training for coaches and judges in strict alignment with national federation rules.

State Selection Trials
Direct qualification pathway for Telangana contingent in National Championships.
Standardized Judging
Certified panel of federation referees, technical scorers, and timekeepers.
