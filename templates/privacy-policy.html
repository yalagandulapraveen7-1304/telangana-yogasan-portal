Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Principles governing participant registration, minor athlete protection, identity verification, and data security under GIGW 3.0 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Commitment to Participant and Minor Privacy

The Telangana Yoga Association prioritizes the privacy, dignity, and digital safety of all sports yogasana athletes, technical officials, district coordinators, and member academies. We adhere strictly to data minimization, purpose limitation, and role-based access control, particularly when managing records of minor athletes.

1 Categories of Data Collected

To administer competitive yogasana championships, sanction athletic divisions, and maintain official state sports records, the association collects only the necessary details:

  • Athlete Demographic Information: Full name, gender, residential district, school or yoga academy affiliation.
  • Age Verification Proof: Date of birth and an official proof document (Municipal Birth Certificate, School Study Bonafide, or Passport).
  • Aadhaar Information (Strictly Limited): Only the last 4 digits of the Aadhaar number are recorded for anti-impersonation cross-verification. Full 12-digit Aadhaar numbers are never stored in databases.
  • Passport-size Photograph: Used solely for hall ticket (admit card) generation and athlete field accreditation.
  • Guardian & Official Contact Information: Parent/guardian name, coach mobile number, and school principal email for emergency contact and tournament coordination.

2 Specific Protections for Minor Athletes (Ages 8 to <18)

Recognizing that the majority of championship participants fall within the Sub-Junior (08–14 yrs) and Junior (14–18 yrs) divisions, the association enforces specialized safeguards:

Parental / Guardian Consent Requirement: Every nomination submitted via district secretaries or institutional coordinators must confirm explicit parental or legal guardian consent before submission.

Protection of Children's Digital Identity: Uploaded proof documents are restricted to an isolated, non-public storage directory. They are never indexed by web search engines or accessible via public URLs.

Responsible Event Photography: Championship photographs taken during sanctioned competitions capture sporting postures and podium ceremonies. Images are reviewed by the Technical Committee to ensure athlete dignity, and any athlete or guardian may request the retraction of specific gallery photographs.

3 Purpose Limitation & Use of Information

Collected personal data is used exclusively for sanctioned sporting and organizational functions:

Championship Entry & Judging
Assigning unique chest numbers, grouping into age categories, and generating judges' scoring sheets.
Admit Card (Hall Ticket) Generation
Rendering printable athlete hall tickets carrying the participant's photo, chest number, and event schedule.
State Selection & Merit Archives
Publishing medal tallies, district ranking records, and submitting qualified state contingents to the national federation.
No Commercial Sharing
Participant records are never sold, leased, or disclosed to third-party commercial marketing entities.

4 Cybersecurity & Access Controls

Technical protections are implemented across the portal infrastructure in alignment with GIGW 3.0 Section 5.3:

  • Tenant & District Isolation: District secretaries can only access and view athlete rosters belonging to their verified district. Cross-district record retrieval is prevented by server-side authorization guards.
  • Credential Security: Administrative passwords are stored using salted cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) with high work factors. Plaintext passwords are never recorded.
  • Transport Layer Security: All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS and HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS).
  • Injection & Traversal Defense: All incoming form parameters undergo server-side NoSQL operator sanitization, regex escaping, and path-traversal blocking.

5 Data Retention & Archival Policy

Competition performance results, chest numbers, and official merit ranks are preserved as permanent historical sports records. Uploaded verification certificates (DOB proof documents) are retained for the duration of the competitive season and selection audit window, after which they may be archived or securely expunged in accordance with the association's archival procedures.
